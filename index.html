How I approach the work

Connecting user needs, product strategy, and scalable interaction systems.

Make what's happening legible

In high-stakes moments, people need to understand what the product is doing and why, especially when AI is involved.

Keep people in control

Good AI helps people make confident decisions. It doesn't make them quietly on someone's behalf.

Design the system, not the screen

An architecture background taught me to work within constraints and build interaction systems that scale with the product.

About

I take a systems-oriented approach to complex product problems, tying design decisions to the business outcomes they serve.

At Rocket Lawyer I design AI-powered legal experiences across strategy, conversational UX, interaction systems, research, and experimentation. Before that I worked on growth, membership, healthcare, and operational products at companies, startups, and public-sector organizations.
